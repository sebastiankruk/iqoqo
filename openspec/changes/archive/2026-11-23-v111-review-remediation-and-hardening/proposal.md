## Why

The unified Principal + Gemini pre-merge review of `release/1.1.1` (PR #447, documented in `.context/notes/review/1.1.1/principal-review-release-1.1.1.md` and `.context/notes/review/1.1.1/review-release-1.1.1-pr-447.md`) identified 2 Critical blockers, 10 Moderate issues, and 5 Low hygiene items across the `v1.1.1` patch workstreams (`C139`, `C140`, `C141`, `C145`, and `#451`). Left unfixed, `make start preview prebuilt` fails with a container `UID 10001` permission error on `0600` `.env` bind-mounts and skips `preview-reconcile`, `GraphService.build_library_graph()` exhausts all 150 node slots on `Work` rows alone (producing 0 edges and starving all other entity tiers on production-scale catalogs), accepted friends' `public` items disappear when the owner sets `FederationConsent.federated_collection == False`, and legacy format normalization/filtering leaves gaps across single-column backfills, `scripts/fix_physical_kinds.py`, PostgreSQL FTS `NULL`/case handling, and wishlist share queries. Resolving all Critical, Moderate, and Low findings in `C146` ensures `v1.1.1` ships cleanly and passes release verification.

## What Changes

- **Layer 1 — DevOps, `Makefile` & Preview Env Hardening (`C139` / `C145` Remediation — 1 Critical, 1 Moderate, 2 Low):**
  - **[CRITICAL]** Fix `Makefile` `start` (`prebuilt` preview branch, `Makefile:345-380`): resolve `COMPOSE_ENV_FILE` safely for both relative and absolute paths (avoiding `$(CURDIR)/$(COMPOSE_ENV_FILE)` double-prefixing), stream or modify `.env` without failing on host `0600` ownership inside the `UID 10001` (`appuser`) `web` container, and invoke `preview-reconcile` (`reconcile_preview_settings.py` + OpenObserve 401/403 credential check/reset) so `make start preview prebuilt` achieves true parity with `make preview-up` and `scripts/iqopretest.sh`.
  - **[MODERATE]** Fix `Makefile` `monitoring-reset-auth` (`Makefile:590-611`): pipe `OPENOBSERVE_BASIC_AUTH` via `stdin` (`sys.stdin.read()`) into `docker compose exec -T web python3 -c ...` instead of passing `-e OO_BASIC_AUTH="$$oo_auth"` in the process argument vector (`ps aux`), and log actionable warnings on database sync errors instead of silently swallowing failures.
  - **[LOW]** Harden `.env` variable extraction and path resolution across `Makefile` targets (`Makefile:355, 362, 488, 602`): handle absolute `COMPOSE_ENV_FILE` paths cleanly without `./$(COMPOSE_ENV_FILE)` prefixing and extract specific env keys safely without fragile shell sourcing side effects.
  - **[LOW]** Ensure consistent `IQOQO_AI_MODE=1` quiet flag propagation across any remaining `Makefile` helper sub-invocations.
- **Layer 2 — FRBR Knowledge Graph & SPARQL Service (`C140` Remediation — 1 Critical, 2 Moderate, 1 Low):**
  - **[CRITICAL]** Fix `GraphService.build_library_graph()` (`app/core/graph_service.py:450-585`): replace sequential greedy `effective_max_nodes` allocation (where Step 1 `Work` consumes all 150 node slots on catalogs with `>= 150` works and starves `Contributor`, `Expression`, `Manifestation`, `Map`, `Item`, and `LOD` tiers) with a balanced multi-tier budget that seeds connected child `Expression`, `Manifestation`, `Item`, `Person`, `Map`, and `LOD` nodes for the selected `Work` / `Series` roots; push `Work.work_kind != "series"` into the SQL `WHERE` clause prior to `LIMIT` when `want_work and not want_series`; and query/emit `WorkPart`, `WorkExpansionLink`, `ContainerAggregation`, `ExpressionContribution`, and `ManifestationContribution` edges in Step 7 whenever their endpoint nodes and relationship families (`parts_expansions`, `contributions`) are active.
  - **[MODERATE]** Bound `GraphService.build_neighborhood()` per-node fan-out at `depth >= 3` (`app/core/graph_service.py:213, 229-388`): enforce a deterministic per-node neighbor expansion cap (`MAX_NEIGHBORS_PER_NODE`) so high-degree hubs at hop 1 cannot greedily exhaust `MAX_GRAPH_NODES` in FIFO order or trigger unbounded round-trip query fan-out at depth 3–4.
  - **[MODERATE]** Fix `FederationConsent` friend visibility parity (`app/core/graph_service.py:79-85, 517-527` & `app/core/sparql_service.py:628-642`): exempt accepted friends (`viewer_friend_ids`) from the `opted_out_user_ids` exclusion when evaluating `Item.visibility == "public"` in `is_item_visible_to_viewer()`, `GraphService.build_library_graph()`, and `SparqlService._build_rdf_graph()`, so opting out of public federation/graph listing does not hide a friend's `public` items from accepted friends who can already see their `friends_only` items.
  - **[LOW]** Consolidate the SQL item visibility + `FederationConsent` friend-exemption predicate into a shared helper used by both `GraphService` and `SparqlService`.
- **Layer 3 — Media Format Normalization, Migration, CLI & Public Share Filtering (`C141` Remediation — 5 Moderate, 1 Low):**
  - **[MODERATE]** Fix Alembic migration `migrations/versions/v1_1_1_norm_legacy_formats.py` (`_resolve_target_format()`, lines 55–98): detect partial column population (`has_missing_column` when any of `format`, `format_type`, or `meta['format']` is `NULL`/blank while another column holds a canonical format) so single-column legacy rows backfill all three columns consistently; and invoke `FormatNormalizer.recover_carrier_from_raw_payload(raw_payload, content_type=content_type)` when all three format columns are `NULL`/blank and `raw_payload` is present.
  - **[MODERATE]** Fix `scripts/fix_physical_kinds.py` (`lines 80-101, 383-417`): update `get_non_canonical_rows()` to detect cross-column drift (`Manifestation.format != Manifestation.format_type` or any of `format`, `format_type`, `meta['format']` containing a non-canonical or more specific carrier alias such as `format="dvd"` + `format_type="płyta vcd"`), pass `content_type` into `FormatNormalizer.recover_carrier_from_raw_payload()`, and repair mis-tagged `Expression.content_type = 'music'` rows when unambiguous `dvd`, `bluray`, `vcd`, or `book` carriers are recovered (matching `v1_1_1_norm_legacy_formats.py`).
  - **[MODERATE]** Fix `UserWorkIntent` wishlist branch and FTS format exclusion ordering in `app/api/public_items.py` (`lines 468-520, 973-998`): constrain the wishlist `sa.exists(...)` subquery by bound `UserWorkIntent.expression_id` and `UserWorkIntent.manifestation_id` when set, apply `filter_specs["include_null_fmt"]` and `filter_specs["excluded_formats"]` to wishlist shares, and push `excluded_formats` into `SearchService.search_manifestations()` / `search_items()` before the SQL `LIMIT 500` cap so top-ranked excluded-format hits cannot crowd out valid results.
  - **[MODERATE]** Fix PostgreSQL FTS (`_pg_manifestation_fts`, `_pg_item_fts` in `app/core/search_service.py:371-376, 543-548`) and `CatalogFilterBuilder._apply_format` (`app/api/filters.py:286-309`): wrap format comparisons in `LOWER(COALESCE(m.format, m.format_type, m.meta ->> 'format'))` and include `OR COALESCE(m.format, m.format_type, m.meta ->> 'format') IS NULL` when `filter_includes_null_format(format_filter)` is true.
  - **[MODERATE]** Fix `validate_format_type()` in `app/core/f3_validation.py` (`lines 270-295`) and `DataManager.get_filtered_works()` in `app/core/data_manager.py` (`lines 185-204`): include `"moving image"` in `generic_category_tokens`, automatically normalize explicit carrier aliases (e.g. `"płyta dvd"`, `"płyta vcd"`, `"książka papierowa"`, `"lp"`) via `FormatNormalizer` when `strict=False`, and update `DataManager.get_filtered_works()` to evaluate `func.lower(func.coalesce(Manifestation.format, Manifestation.format_type, Manifestation.meta["format"].as_string()))` with `filter_includes_null_format()` support.
  - **[LOW]** Pre-compute lowercased alias reverse lookups in `FormatNormalizer` (`app/utils/format_normalizer.py`) when the YAML mapping cache loads to eliminate per-query dict iteration overhead in `expand_format_filter()`.
- **Layer 4 — Frontend `/graph` UX Density, i18n & Release Documentation (`C140`, `C141` Remediation — 2 Moderate, 1 Low):**
  - **[MODERATE]** Compact `/graph?mode=library` entity-type filter bar in `frontend/app/graph/page.tsx` (`lines 144-160`): render at most 4 primary FRBR entity-type toggle pills inline (`work`, `expression`, `manifestation`, `item`) and group the 4 secondary node domains (`series`, `contributor`, `map`, `lod`) behind a compact disclosure/popover control so visible inline button density obeys the `<= 4` control-group rule.
  - **[MODERATE]** Populate `## [1.1.1] - 2026-11-23` (`Added`, `Changed`, `Fixed`) in `docs/CHANGELOG.md` covering `C139`, `C140`, `C141` (including `vcd` carrier support and Polish carrier normalization), `C145`, `C146`, and `#451` (`nginx:1.31.9`), and update `.context/notes/plan/1.1.x-release-plan.md` and `.context/notes/iqoqo development notes.md`.
  - **[LOW]** Verify and ensure complete `en.json` and sentence-case `pl.json` localization keys for the new `/graph` compact entity filter disclosure and `vcd` carrier format badges.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
_None — all behavioral refinements strengthen existing canonical specifications._

### Modified Capabilities
- `prebuilt-env-propagation-and-secret-decrypt-guard`: Ensure `make start preview prebuilt` handles `0600` `.env` permissions without container `UID 10001` bind-mount `PermissionError`, supports both relative and absolute `COMPOSE_ENV_FILE` paths, and executes `preview-reconcile` (`reconcile_preview_settings.py` + OpenObserve 401/403 auth recovery).
- `openobserve-telemetry-and-rum-resilience`: Require `monitoring-reset-auth` to pass `OPENOBSERVE_BASIC_AUTH` via `stdin` rather than process argv (`-e OO_BASIC_AUTH`) when syncing `InstanceSettings`.
- `visual-frbr-relation-graph`: Require `GraphService.build_library_graph()` to partition `max_nodes` across active entity tiers and seed connected FRBR/contributor/map/LOD subgraphs without starving non-`Work` nodes on `>= 150`-work catalogs, push `Work.work_kind != "series"` into SQL before `LIMIT`, emit `WorkPart`/`WorkExpansionLink`/`ContainerAggregation`/`ExpressionContribution`/`ManifestationContribution` edges in library mode, bound per-node neighbor expansion at `depth >= 3`, exempt accepted friends from `FederationConsent` public item exclusion, and keep `/graph?mode=library` inline entity-type filter buttons `<= 4` via a compact secondary filter disclosure.
- `sparql-federated-access-rights`: Exempt accepted friends from `FederationConsent.federated_collection == False` exclusion on `public` items across `SparqlService` and `GraphService` via a shared SQL visibility predicate helper.
- `format-normalization`: Require PostgreSQL FTS (`_pg_manifestation_fts`, `_pg_item_fts`), `CatalogFilterBuilder._apply_format`, and `DataManager.get_filtered_works` to apply case-insensitive (`LOWER(COALESCE(...))`) and `NULL`-inclusive (`filter_includes_null_format`) format matching with pre-`LIMIT` `excluded_formats` support, and require `f3_validation.validate_format_type` to normalize `"moving image"` and explicit carrier aliases when `strict=False`.
- `format-mapping-cli`: Require `migrations/versions/v1_1_1_norm_legacy_formats.py` and `scripts/fix_physical_kinds.py` to detect and backfill single-column / cross-column format drift (including `format="dvd"` vs `format_type="płyta vcd"`), recover carriers from `raw_payload` when all three format columns are `NULL`, and repair mis-tagged `Expression.content_type = 'music'` rows when unambiguous `dvd`, `bluray`, `vcd`, or `book` carriers are recovered.
- `shared-collection-type-integrity`: Require `UserWorkIntent` wishlist share filtering in `_query_public_share_items` to honor bound `expression_id` and `manifestation_id` foreign keys alongside `include_null_fmt` and `excluded_formats`, and enforce `excluded_formats` inside FTS queries prior to `LIMIT`.
